Reading policy

Booking Policy

Effective September 3, 2026

This policy covers personal tarot readings booked through AlyHackbart.com.

Service and delivery

General Tarot costs $15 for a Quick Pull, $35 for a Full Reading, or $60 for a Live Reading. Love and Money and Career readings cost $20 for one question, $40 for a written deep dive, or $65 for a live reading.

Written readings are delivered to the email used at checkout within 3 business days. Live readings are 30 minutes and scheduled by email based on mutual availability.

Refunds

You may request a full refund before Aly begins preparing your personalized reading. Once preparation has begun or a reading has been delivered, the purchase is not refundable.

Rescheduling

One reschedule is available for a live reading when requested at least 24 hours before the appointment. Email Aly as soon as possible if you need to change your time.

Personal reflection

Readings are for personal reflection and entertainment only. They are not medical, legal, financial, or mental health advice and are not a substitute for support from a qualified professional.

Support contributions

Support Aly payments are optional personal contributions. They are not charitable donations, and no goods or services are provided in exchange.

Questions or requests

For a refund request, rescheduling request, or question about a booking, email alysonhackbart@gmail.com and include the email address used at checkout.
