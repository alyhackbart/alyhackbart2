Frequently asked questions

The practical details, before you book.

These are Aly's current planning defaults. Every project receives a written scope with its exact deliverables, schedule, and terms.

How long does delivery take?

Short social edits usually take about 5 business days. Filmed content and event packages usually take 7 to 14 business days. Larger projects and rush requests receive a custom schedule.

How many revisions are included?

The listed packages include one consolidated revision round. Additional revisions or a major change in direction are quoted separately.

Is a deposit required?

A booking retainer is usually required to reserve a filming date or begin a larger edit. The amount and payment schedule are shown in the written proposal.

How far outside San Diego do you travel?

Local San Diego projects are the primary focus. Travel, parking, permits, and projects outside the immediate area are quoted based on the location and schedule.

Do I receive the raw footage?

Raw footage is not included unless it is listed in the approved scope. A raw footage handoff can be added when storage, organization, and usage terms are agreed in advance.

Are captions included?

Basic captions are included when they are listed in the package. Custom typography, detailed animation, or advanced motion graphics are separate add-ons.

Who handles music licensing?

When music is included, Aly uses properly licensed music that fits the agreed use. Tell Aly before the project if the video will run as a paid advertisement or needs broader commercial usage.

What happens if a shoot needs to be rescheduled?

Contact Aly as soon as possible. The written proposal confirms the rescheduling and cancellation terms for that project, including any non-recoverable costs.
