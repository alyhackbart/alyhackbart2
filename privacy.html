Website policy

Privacy Notice

Effective September 3, 2026

How AlyHackbart.com handles information submitted through the website.

Information you submit

The contact form may collect your name, email address, project type, timeline, budget range, optional project link, and project details. If you purchase a reading, Stripe may also collect the reading format you choose and the details needed to provide the reading, such as an area of focus or birth information. Please do not submit information that is not needed for your request.

How the information is used

Aly uses submitted information to review and respond to inquiries, discuss potential work, and keep a record of project communication.

Form processing

The contact form is processed by FormSubmit. Information entered in the form is transmitted to that service and delivered to Aly by email.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Stripe receives the payment and billing information needed to complete the transaction. Aly does not receive your full card number. Information submitted at checkout is used to confirm payment, provide the purchased service, arrange scheduling, and respond to payment questions.

Analytics and technical logs

This website does not currently use advertising cookies or behavioral analytics. Basic technical logs may still be created by browsers, networks, hosting providers, or security systems.

External links

This website links to Stripe checkout and may link to other third-party websites. Those services may process technical information according to their own policies.

Questions

For privacy questions or to request deletion of an inquiry, email alysonhackbart@gmail.com.
