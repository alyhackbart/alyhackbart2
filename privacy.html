Privacy Notice

Effective August 31, 2026

This notice explains how Aly Hackbart handles information submitted through AlyHackbart.com. It is a practical description of current website practices and is not a substitute for legal advice.

Information you choose to provide

The project inquiry form may collect your name, email address, phone number, business or organization, project type, approximate budget, preferred date, project location, and project details.

How the information is used

The information is used to respond to your inquiry, evaluate project fit, prepare a quote or proposal, schedule work, and communicate about services you requested. It is not sold or rented for advertising.

Form processing and hosting

Form submissions are transmitted through FormSubmit, which forwards the submission to alysonhackbart@gmail.com. The website is hosted using Google Cloud infrastructure. Service providers may process technical information needed to deliver, secure, and operate their services.

Analytics and cookies

This website does not currently use advertising cookies or behavioral analytics. A basic session may still involve technical logs created by browsers, networks, hosting providers, or security systems.

Retention

Inquiry emails and related correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a potential or active project, maintain business records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. You may ask for deletion of information that is no longer needed, subject to legitimate recordkeeping requirements.

Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers used to operate the website and deliver messages, or when disclosure is reasonably required by law, safety, fraud prevention, or the protection of legal rights. Information is not shared with third parties for unrelated marketing.

Security

Reasonable steps are used to protect inquiry information, but no internet transmission or email system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

This website is intended for business and project inquiries and is not directed to children under 13. Do not submit personal information for a child through the inquiry form.

Your choices

You may contact Aly to ask what inquiry information is held, request a correction, or request deletion when applicable. You can also avoid the form and email Aly directly.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated as website tools or business practices change. The effective date at the top will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

Questions or requests about this notice can be sent to alysonhackbart@gmail.com.
